# Design log: Redline

Changes made after the design brief was approved, newest last.

- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard:** 30 x 20 cm at 55 cm became 18 x 12 cm held at waist height (45 cm ahead, 45 cm below eye level, tilted up toward the face), with the density value twice as big and a larger button label. *Why:* in the preview the clipboard covered most of the view, hiding the crowd and the red floor. The 28 cm below eye level I first asked for put it 32° below the horizon, exactly where the highlighted square sits from the starting spot, so it went down to 45 cm, a true waist height. *Checked:* from the starting spot, looking at the square, the clipboard sits in the bottom of the view below the square and doesn't cover it.
- **2026-11-09 · Ghosts:** translucent, light figures became dim, solid, unlit blue-grey (#6E8BA8) figures with real proportions (about 44 cm shoulders, 28 cm deep), a distinct round head on a narrow neck, a slightly brighter shoulder line and crown, and each turned up to 25° at random. *Why:* at 5 per m² the translucent white figures merged into a white haze, so you couldn't see separate people.
- **2026-11-09 · Sway:** each figure shifts its weight a little; at 6 to 7 per m² a slow shared wave runs through the crowd toward the stage. *Why:* above about 5 per m² a real crowd starts moving as one, and seeing that makes the redline something you feel.
- **2026-11-09 · Crowd murmur:** a spatial crowd murmur from the middle of the zone, louder as the density rises. *Why:* sound makes the crowd feel present even when it's out of view.
- **2026-11-09 · LEAF tests:** 6 scenarios (one per done criterion, plus sway and murmur), all passing in the preview. The slider is set through its UI Kit value because LEAF 2.0.2 can't drag a UI Kit slider knob, so the mouse drag stays a manual check.
- **2026-11-09 · Review fixes (no design change, the build now matches the brief):** ghosts stay at least 22 cm (half a shoulder width) inside the zone outline, so none sticks out past it; the ghosts load in their blue-grey again (the scene had saved them black, which shows as see-through on the glasses); the zone and the clipboard keep facing the right way when you look straight down at the floor; after Edit zone the floor reads "0 people" instead of a count for a crowd that isn't shown. Ghosts now share one material pair (each gets its own only while fading) and the zone-in-view check samples just the outline, which cuts per-frame work on the glasses. *Checked:* type-check clean; a layout check over zones from 2 x 2 m to 8 x 6 m at every density gives the exact count with no body over the outline. Not yet re-run in the preview or with LEAF (Lens Studio MCP was offline). *Before publishing:* disable the "LEAF Plugin Bridge" and LeafIndex objects so the test scenarios don't ship in the Lens.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard type hierarchy:** the hint ("Fit the zone, then pinch Done") moved from enlarged body text to the caption size in 60% white, so the panel reads density value (largest), then the button label, then the hint (smallest). *Why:* the hint was nearly as big as the button label, so nothing told the eye what mattered first.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard spacing:** even 0.5 cm gaps became grouped ones: 0.4 cm between the value and the slider (they read as one control), 0.9 cm before the hint and 0.5 cm before the button. The rows exactly fill the 18 x 12 cm plate, so the panel stays the same size.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard hit areas:** the slider's touch area is 3.6 cm tall (the track is still 2 cm) and the button's is 12 x 4 cm (the face is still 11 x 3 cm). The two areas stay 1.1 cm apart, so a pinch never lands on both. *Why:* Specs asks for targets of at least 6 cm at 110 cm, about 3.5 cm at the clipboard's ~64 cm, and the slider was 2 cm.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard motion:** when the Lens places it, the panel rises 2 cm and grows from 94% to full size over 0.35 s, eased out. The hint fades in over 0.25 s and out over 0.15 s, and the button label cross-fades between Done and Edit zone. A change mid-fade picks up from where it is. *Why:* the hint and label used to snap, so the switch from setup to done felt abrupt.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard polish, checks:** type-check clean. Preview screenshot and LEAF re-run still to do: the Lens Studio MCP server was offline.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard level caption:** under the density value, a plain-language caption in the floor's colour: "Room to move" at 1 to 2, "Getting tight" at 3 to 4, "Past the redline" at 5 to 7, nothing at 0. It's greyed out with the value during setup. To fit it, the value row went from 2.6 to 2.2 cm and the gaps tightened, and the panel grew from 18 x 12 to 18 x 12.5 cm. The slider and button touch areas still keep 0.7 cm apart. *Why:* a number alone ("4 per m²") doesn't say what it means. The words do, at a glance. *Checked:* type-check clean, and the C3 LEAF scenario now also checks the caption at every level from 1 to 7; it passes in the preview.
- **2026-11-09 · Setup hint wording:** "Fit the zone, then pinch Done" became "Fit the zone to your space, then pinch Done". It still shows only while the slider is greyed out and fades away after Done.
- **2026-11-09 · Font and button icons, not done yet:** a bold, highly readable font for the value and floor labels, and icons on the Done / Edit zone button (the Icons package already has `checkmark` and `pencil-line`). Both need the Lens Studio MCP server to import the assets, and it was offline. Preview check and LEAF re-run also still to do.
- **2026-11-09 · Slider ticks:** each slider step now plays a soft, short click (80 ms). Going down it's 2 semitones lower and about 4 dB quieter than going up. Each direction has its own player, so a quick reversal doesn't cut the other tick off, and both are set to low latency because they answer the hand. Ticks play only for slider moves, not for test hooks or resets. *Why:* you can feel each step without looking at the number, and a step back sounds like easing off.
- **2026-11-09 · Calm redline tone:** the redline tone was re-made as a low, calm swell: G3 (196 Hz) with a soft octave and a quiet fifth, a 140 ms fade-in, a slow decay and a little room reverb, about 3 s with the tail. It still plays once each time the density reaches 5 from below. *Why:* it should feel like a weight settling, not an alarm. A steward hearing an alarm reads it as "something broke". It stays at G3 instead of going lower because small glasses speakers barely reproduce below ~150 Hz. *Checked:* type-check clean. New LEAF scenario R8 checks one tone going 4 → 5, none for 6, 7 and back to 5, and one tick per step in each direction. R8 and all other LEAF scenarios pass in the preview.
- **2026-11-09 · Test your eye mode:** a second button on the clipboard, Test your eye, starts a practice round. The zone fills at a random whole density from 2 to 7 (never the same twice in a row), and everything that gives the number away is hidden: the value reads "Guess: N", the level caption asks "How dense is it?", and the floor tint, the square's glowing feet and number, and the short floor count go away. The crowd, its murmur and the full floor labels stay. The slider sets the guess, with ticks but no redline tone. Check reveals the true value in its colour, the line "You guessed 4, it was 6", and a small top-down map in place of the slider: the zone's outline with one dot per person, the stage edge at the top. Next round re-rolls; Stop test goes back to the density set before. The two buttons sit side by side, 8 x 3 cm each, with touch areas 0.2 cm apart. Test your eye is greyed out until Done. *Why:* reading density is a skill stewards need without a number on the wall, and a guess with a reveal trains it faster than watching the slider. *Checked:* type-check clean. New LEAF scenario R9 runs a whole round (hidden state, guess, Check, map dot count, Next round, Stop test), and done criterion 6 was added to the brief. All 9 LEAF scenarios pass in the preview; R9's live run hid a density of 6 (72 ghosts, no tint), and a guess of 4 read back "You guessed 4, it was 6" with the floor turning red. The look of the button row and the map isn't checked yet: the connected Lens Studio MCP server offered no screenshot tool.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard in view and see-through:** the clipboard moved from waist height (45 cm below eye level, where Specs' ~37°-tall view meant looking down to read it) to about 55 cm away and 10° below the line of sight. It follows head turns and nods lazily, so it's in view whether you look ahead or down at the square. The backing dropped to 30% opacity and stopped writing depth, so the crowd shows through it; text, slider and buttons stay solid. Both the angle and the opacity are Inspector settings. *Why:* "I don't have to look down for the dashboard": reading the number shouldn't take your eyes off the crowd.
- **2026-11-09 · Font and button icons:** the density value and the floor labels use Archivo Black, a single heavy weight with clear digits. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next was tried first, but the import only brings in its regular weight. The floor labels' size maths now uses Archivo's cap height (0.688). Each button has a Material icon that cross-fades with its label: check (Done, Check), edit (Edit zone), arrow_forward (Next round), visibility (Test your eye) and stop_circle (Stop test). `close` and `refresh` were the first picks, but they never landed on disk.
- **2026-11-09 · Checks after font, icons and see-through clipboard:** TypeScript compiles in Lens Studio and all 9 LEAF scenarios pass in the preview (C1 to C5, R6 to R9). The look (transparency, the clipboard staying in view, icon placement on the buttons) has no screenshot check yet, because the connected MCP server offers no screenshot tool.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard clicks blocked by template demo:** after the clipboard moved up into view, clicks stopped reaching it. The log showed the template's `[Manipulate Example] Box` reacting instead. The project template's `[EXAMPLES] Rocket Workshop` group is still switched on, and its Menu and InteractiveTransform sit about 50 cm ahead, closer than the clipboard's 55 cm, so they catch the click first. *Fix:* switch off `[EXAMPLES] Rocket Workshop` in the Scene Hierarchy (it isn't part of Redline). The LEAF tests didn't catch it because they trigger buttons directly instead of aiming a ray at them.
- **2026-11-09 · People in different shades:** each ghost is now one of five shades of the blue-grey: darker and bluer, the base colour, lighter and warmer, slightly greener, and lightest. Shades are spread evenly through the crowd, so there are no stripes. Each shade is one shared material pair, so the crowd still batches. *Why:* identical figures read as a pattern, and different shades read as separate people.
- **2026-11-09 · Crowd across the whole room:** the zone's size cap went from 8 x 6 m to 10 x 10 m, so the corner handles can stretch it over a whole room, and the slider sets the density across all of it. The 4 x 3 m start stays, because the done criteria and LEAF tests are built on it. At 10 x 10 m and 7 per m² that's 700 ghosts, so frame time on glasses needs checking.
- **2026-11-09 · Clipboard made clickable and more robust:** clicks on the clipboard weren't reaching its buttons. An injected click exactly on the Done button reached no button at all. Three changes: (1) the panel holds still while the pointer is on any of its controls (buttons, slider, Pin), because it used to keep following the view and could slide out from under a pinch; (2) it rides higher and a little farther, 5° below the line of sight at 60 cm instead of 10° at 55 cm, because the Done button sat at 91% of the screen height, right at the edge of the view; (3) a new round Pin button in the top-right corner fixes the clipboard in place (icon bright when pinned), and pinching again lets it follow. Correction: an earlier entry blamed the template's `[EXAMPLES]` demo for catching clicks, but that whole group is under `SIKExamples`, which is already switched off, so it wasn't the cause. *Checked:* type-check clean. Not yet clicked in the preview: Lens Studio crashed at 22:32 while re-importing the script, after its preview had been hanging since about 22:20.
